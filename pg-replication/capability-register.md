# Capability register: Phase 1 (two Postgres nodes, WAL streaming)

What this experiment has **proved**, with the evidence for each claim, and what it has **not**. A capability is listed
as proven only if it has been run and the result observed. Last evidence run: **2026-11-21** (sections 7 to 12); hard-kill evidence **2026-11-20** (section 6); earlier evidence **2026-11-14**.
One machine (Docker Compose, macOS), PostgreSQL 16, two nodes `pg-primary` and `pg-standby`.

**Status legend**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Proven (auto)** | Checked by a command that can be re-run and gives PASS or FAIL: `run-all-checks.sh`, `verify-replication.sh`, or one of the experiment runners listed below |
| **Proven (manual)** | Run by hand and the result recorded below; re-runnable, but no automatic pass/fail yet |
| **Partial** | The main behaviour is shown, with a stated limit that matters |
| **Not proven** | Not tested. See "Gaps" |

Re-run the fast checks: `bash scripts/run-all-checks.sh` (lint of all 31 shell files, the documents check, then the 17-step round trip; about 50 s).

The experiments change the databases and are slower, so they are separate runners, each one PASS or FAIL, each restoring the starting state:
`test-hard-kill.sh --target roundtrip|restore|rotate` (minutes), `test-divergence-rewind.sh` (about 20 s), `test-replica-lag.sh` (about 35 s),
`test-slot-limit.sh` (about 2.5 minutes, writes 1.5 GB of WAL, needs 6 GB free), `test-rotation.sh` (about 16 s) and
`test-network-faults.sh` (the replication link under delay, a cut and silence, about 5 minutes), `test-load.sh` (concurrent load, about 50 s) and `test-maintenance-lock.sh` (about 25 s),
`measure-recovery.sh` (RPO and RTO, 21 trials of about 30 s, about 13 minutes; `--scenarios crash-matrix` adds 12 trials, about 10 minutes; it writes [docs/recovery-report.md](docs/recovery-report.md)).

Closed gaps keep their numbers out of section 4. Where each went: GAP-01 to RPO-01, GAP-02 to RPO-02, GAP-04 to FOV-09,
GAP-05 to RTO-01, GAP-06 to CRS-01, GAP-08 to DEC-01, GAP-09 to OPS-04, GAP-13 to SEC-R2, GAP-14 to SEC-R3.

| DET-01 | **The failure detector decides from two vantage points and does not act on a pause or a one-sided partition.** A detect-only controller (`services/failover_controller`, Python) probes the writer through Toxiproxy and asks the standby whether the writer still feeds it; it says `fail-over` only after 3 failed probes in a row **and** a standby that hears nothing | **Proven (auto, detect-only)**: `test-detector.sh`; 8 unit tests of the pure detector | 2026-11-22, two full runs, all 7 steps passed.
